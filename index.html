🗺️ Master Roadmap

Step 1: The Foundation (Months 1-3)

Read Class 6-12 NCERTs like a storybook. Do not make notes yet. Read The Hindu daily.


Step 2: Core Subjects (Months 4-8)

Start standard books like Laxmikanth for Polity. Begin making short notes.


Step 3: Mains & Optional (Months 9-10)

Complete Optional subject. Write 2 Mains answers daily.


Step 4: Prelims Mode (Months 11-12)

Stop all Mains prep. Focus 100% on Prelims MCQs and CSAT.
